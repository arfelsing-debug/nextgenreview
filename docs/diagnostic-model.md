# Diagnostic model

The Review measures individual readiness, not family-system maturity.

## Five interpretive lenses

1. Awareness: does the participant understand the issue?
2. Alignment: are personal intentions and family expectations understood?
3. Capability: does the participant possess relevant knowledge and skill?
4. Experience: has the participant practised the responsibility?
5. Resilience: could the participant act effectively under pressure or disruption?

## Participant-facing classifications

- Established
- Developing
- Exposed
- Unclear

These are interpretive classifications, not personality labels and not a simplistic overall score.

## Report outputs

The report should identify strongest foundations, developing areas, knowledge gaps, expectation gaps, capability-versus-experience gaps, responsibilities likely to arrive before readiness, dependencies, three priorities and a practical 12-month readiness plan.

Core closing question:

> What should I be able to understand, discuss or do twelve months from now that I cannot confidently do today?
