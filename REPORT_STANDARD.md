# Adamas report standard

Effective 4 October 2026. Applies to Family Continuity, NextGen Readiness, Shareholder Readiness, Adviser Coordination and every future Adamas review.

Every complete report contains one cover and exactly 17 numbered report pages. The signed introductory letter occupies page 1. Online and downloadable PDF reports use the same respondent-specific findings and priorities. English, Czech and German have equivalent depth and the same visual treatment.

## Depth of explanation

The second substantive paragraph of the opening letter is an expanded summary, approximately twice the detail of the earlier short version. It identifies reported strengths or their absence, the three priorities, their practical significance, the first course of action and the 30-day, 90-day and annual review horizons. It remains on the signed letter page.

The remainder of the report should be substantially fuller in useful interpretation and practical guidance. Explain what the answers suggest, why it matters, what remains uncertain and how the respondent can confirm it. Each dimension addresses its four subdimensions. Carry the same three priorities through the immediate plan, annual plan and working record.

For each priority, give a concrete action, appropriate participants or support, a named lead to be agreed, completion evidence, a review date or horizon and a route forward if blocked. Strong results call for practical confirmation and an appropriate deeper test. Unknown or inapplicable answers call for clarification of visibility or relevance, without inventing a weakness or assigning a middle score.

Add detail where it helps a reader understand or act. Preserve diagnostic caution, authorised access and the original scoring. Avoid generic repetition, unsupported conclusions and padding solely to fill space. The portrait page may remain mainly visual.

## Consistent presentation

Use the established Adamas cream, green and gold treatment and approved typography, question styles and traffic-light colours. Preserve the cover, signed letter, six-dimension profile and established chapter sequence. Translate all guidance and labels before releasing a language.

The 17-page requirement is a content and layout constraint. It is not permission to compress body text below the approved readable size, clip content, remove useful findings or silently create extra pages. Resolve overflow by editing for clarity and improving layout. Retain meaningful space for notes where provided.

## Release checks

Check strong, developing, weak, unknown, inapplicable and mixed response patterns in every language. Verify cover plus 17 pages, the complete single-page signed letter, all dimension findings and the same three priorities throughout. Check font size, margins, clipping and overlap in the generated PDF. Inspect all final pages and the actual online report. Confirm online/PDF equivalence and record deployment status separately from source or specimen completion.

New review templates must adopt this standard from their first complete draft. Existing reports must be brought to the same standard before their next release.
